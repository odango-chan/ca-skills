Composed node identity for 5gc-file-transfer (ca-skills#7 / ca-skill-toolchain#102).
Convention: ComposedNodeID(siteID, callee, child) = "<siteID>-<callee>-<child>".
Top-level sites splice dependent terminals onto the parent transitions (terminals not emitted).
Loop-body sites keep dependent terminals inside the body; the enclosing for.on routes them.
